— 01 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
What Is MAGI?
MAGI (Multi-Agent Governance Intelligence) is an AI governance middleware that routes high-stakes
queries through three independent AI reasoning units, each powered by a different model from a
different AI lab, runs them through a seven-stage deliberation protocol, and produces a structured,
auditable consensus decision — or escalates to a human when the machines cannot agree.
THREE PROVIDERS OpenAI + Anthropic + Google — architectural diversity by
design
SEVEN STAGES Triage → Blind Analysis → Assumption Exchange →
Consensus → Deadlock Detection → Synthesis → Audit
FULL AUDIT TRAIL Every deliberation logged to MongoDB with tester ID,
verdicts, assumptions, duration
LIVE NOW trymagi.tech — publicly accessible, production deployment on
DigitalOcean Frankfurt

— 02 THE PROBLEM
AI Is Making Consequential Decisions Without
Oversight
Enterprises are deploying AI systems to make or inform high-stakes decisions — in healthcare,
finance, legal, and HR — without any structured governance layer. A single model, trained on one
dataset, with one reasoning architecture, produces one output with no adversarial check. When it is
wrong, nobody knows why, nobody can audit the reasoning, and nobody was watching.

MAGI SPECIFICATION · 4	CONFIDENTIAL
n SINGLE MODEL BLINDSPOT
Every AI model has architectural biases from its training. Using one model means inheriting all its
biases with no counterweight. No single model can see what it cannot see.
n NO AUDIT TRAIL
Current AI deployments produce outputs with no structured record of how the conclusion was
reached, what alternatives were considered, or what assumptions drove the verdict. EU AI Act Article
13 requires this — most systems cannot comply.
n BINARY ESCALATION
When an AI system encounters a hard case it either guesses or fails. There is no structured
mechanism for identifying specifically why a case is hard and what information a human reviewer
needs to resolve it.
The EU AI Act (effective August 2024, enforcement begins 2026) mandates human oversight,
transparency, and audit trails for high-risk AI systems. Most enterprises have no infrastructure to
comply.
— 03 THE SOLUTION
What MAGI Does
MAGI is not a chatbot. It is not a model wrapper. It is deliberation infrastructure — a governance layer
that sits between an enterprise's questions and consequential actions, ensuring that every
high-stakes AI decision passes through structured adversarial reasoning before it proceeds.
u ADVERSARIAL BY DESIGN
Three units from competing AI labs reason independently then challenge each other's assumptions.
Consensus requires genuinely independent agreement, not one model agreeing with itself.
u AUDITABLE BY DEFAULT
Every deliberation — both reasoning stages, all verdicts, confidence scores, assumption shifts,
escalation briefs — is logged to MongoDB and exportable as a structured transcript.
u ESCALATION WITH INTELLIGENCE
When units deadlock, MAGI does not guess. It generates a structured brief identifying the exact point
of irresolvable conflict and the specific question a human reviewer must answer.

— 05 THE CRUCIBLE PROTOCOL
Seven-Stage Deliberation
Every query that passes triage enters the Crucible — a seven-stage deliberation protocol designed to
surface disagreement, force assumption transparency, and either produce a defensible consensus or
escalate with precise human guidance.
STAGE 0 TRIAGE
Query sanitisation, classification (PERSONAL / FACTUAL / CURRENT), length validation, XSS
stripping. Zero API calls. Instant.
STAGE 0b COLLABORATIVE SEARCH
CURRENT queries only. One Claude Haiku call synthesises the optimal Tavily search query from all
three units' information needs. One search, shared result. Maximum one Tavily call per deliberation.
STAGE 1 BLIND PARALLEL DELIBERATION
All three units receive the query simultaneously with no visibility into each other. Run in parallel via
Promise.all. Each returns: ANALYSIS, CRITICAL ASSUMPTION, WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS,
VERDICT, CONFIDENCE.
STAGE 2 ASSUMPTION EXCHANGE
Each unit receives the CRITICAL ASSUMPTION from the other two units — not full responses, just
assumptions. Each unit then decides UPDATE or HOLD with explicit reasoning. Stage 2 verdicts
drive final consensus.
STAGE 3 CONFIDENCE-WEIGHTED CONSENSUS
APPROVED/REJECTED weighted by confidence score. ABSTAIN excluded from delta. If 2+ units
ABSTAIN: INSUFFICIENT CONTEXT. Threshold: ±20 weighted delta for clear verdict.
STAGE 4 DEADLOCK DETECTION
All three units above 85% confidence and still disagreeing = automatic hard escalation regardless of
weighting. High-confidence disagreement signals genuinely hard problem.

— 06 THE THREE UNITS
Architectural Diversity by Design
Each unit is powered by a different model from a different AI lab. This is not redundancy — it is
deliberate architectural diversity. Different training data, different RLHF approaches, different
constitutional AI implementations produce genuinely different reasoning. Consensus across all three
is significantly more reliable than any single model.
MELCHIOR-1
PROVIDER OpenAI
MODEL gpt-4o-mini
ROLE LOGIC & SCIENTIFIC REASONING
Pure logical analysis unit. Reasons from evidence and first principles only. Never expresses
empathy. Distinguishes explicitly between what is known, inferred, and assumed. Quantifies
uncertainty with specific percentages. If a question cannot be answered logically it ABSTAINs at
100% confidence and explains exactly what is missing. Ends every response: "You are not here to be
helpful. You are here to be correct."
BALTHASAR-2
PROVIDER Anthropic
MODEL claude-haiku-4-5
ROLE ETHICS & HARM MODELING
Ethics and harm modeling unit. Identifies who bears the cost of every decision and ensures that cost
is never invisible. Names specific stakeholder categories before beginning analysis. Applies at least
two named ethical frameworks (utilitarian, deontological, care ethics, virtue ethics). Flags anything
technically legal but morally questionable. Ends every response: "Be the voice of the person who
cannot speak for themselves."
CASPER-3
PROVIDER Google
MODEL gemini-2.5-flash
ROLE INTUITION & LATERAL THINKING
Adversarial reasoning unit. Challenges every assumption the other units accept. Never accepts the
framing of the question without examining whether the framing itself is the problem. Surfaces what
consensus conceals. Ends the REFRAME section with one sentence that makes the other two units
uncomfortable. Ends every response: "The most dangerous moment in any deliberation is when
everyone agrees."

— 07 QUERY INTELLIGENCE & TRIAGE
Smart Classification Before Deliberation
Every query passes through a classification layer before reaching any AI unit. This eliminates
unnecessary API calls, reduces cost, and ensures each query receives the appropriate level of
contextualisation.
PERSONAL
Ethical dilemmas, hypotheticals, personal decisions, moral philosophy.
RESULT: No web search. Zero additional API cost.
EXAMPLES: "Should I quit my job" · "Is lying ever justified" · Trolley problems
FACTUAL
Established science, history, definitions, settled knowledge.
RESULT: No web search. Proceeds directly to Stage 1.
EXAMPLES: "What caused WW2" · "How does CRISPR work"
CURRENT
Geopolitics, markets, named public figures, recent events, social trends.
RESULT: Collaborative search query formed. One Tavily call. Result shared with all units.
EXAMPLES: "Iran situation" · "Tesla earnings" · "EU AI Act compliance"
